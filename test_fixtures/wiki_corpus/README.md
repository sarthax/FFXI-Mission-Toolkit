# wiki_corpus

Sanitized Wiki V2 fixtures. `real_manifest.json` holds minimal attributed excerpts of BG Wiki and FFXIclopedia
pages (provenance + full-page SHA-256, never full pages). `synthetic_manifest.json` holds independently authored
samples and must never be cited as source evidence. WikiWikiJP samples are synthetic only. See
`docs/workbench/WIKI_REAL_SOURCE_CORPUS.md`. Do not add full articles, databases or client binaries here.
