Zone Plot

Every mob and NPC spawn row of any zone (live server DB) drawn on the client mesh with the navmesh overlay. Dots are one instanced mesh, so thousands are fine; labels show only for the hovered/selected dots and the nearest ones. Colour = reachable from the anchor over the navmesh (green), disconnected (red), off-mesh (orange), or by kind. Zero-position rows never load in instances and are listed instead of drawn. Shift-click a dot to make it the reachability anchor; click to select. Mesh complexity levels all keep walls, floors and terrain (kept by size, not by type) and only drop small clutter (railings, trim, small props); they swap in real time when you change the dropdown, typically 1-4s. If a level ever looks wrong (holes, missing walls) that is a bug in the LOD triangle filter, not by design — use Legacy OBJ to compare against the untouched geometry.
