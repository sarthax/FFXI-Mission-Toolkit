DAT Inspector

Read-only client resource inspector. Pick a DAT by numeric ID, derive one from a zone and common resource family, or enter a ROM-relative path such as ROM/7/44.DAT. The inspector never modifies client files.

DAT ID

Use this when another tool, server record, or capture already gave you a DAT ID.

Zone resource

Pick a zone and one of the common per-zone DAT families. The numeric DAT ID is calculated for you.


Client DAT path

Use a path inside the configured FFXI client. Relative paths are resolved from the client root.
